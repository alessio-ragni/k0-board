What it does

Six things, each one because juggling sessions without it was costing an afternoon.

Seven colours, one glance

Waiting for an answer, a plan to approve, your turn, working, done. The board sorts itself by who needs you first.

The real terminal, one click away

No embedded imitation. Double-click a note and the session's own terminal comes to the front.

Decisions that get checked

Epics, stories and every decision taken while discussing them — then the finished work held up against each one before anything is called done.

One number, everywhere

K150 is the same story in every repository and on every machine. Offline, a story waits with a provisional key and gets its real one when you are back.

Your board on your phone

k0 Cloud keeps a copy, so the board survives a lost laptop and reads from any browser.

Work in a worktree, merge back

A planned story is built in its own git worktree, logged as it goes, and merged back into the branch it came from.

Three steps

From nothing to a board full of sessions in about a minute.

  1. 01 Install it npx k0-board — it runs on your machine, next to Claude Code.
  2. 02 Sign in GitHub or Google. Your board syncs to k0 Cloud and keeps working offline.
  3. 03 Open ten sessions Start them from the board and stop wondering which one needed you.
