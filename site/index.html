The board that knows which session needs you.

Every Claude Code session is a sticky note. It turns red when it is waiting for an answer, orange when a plan needs your yes, and green when it is done. Double-click and you are in its terminal. Stories, epics and the decisions behind them live on the same board — and follow you to every machine.

Three steps

From nothing to a board full of sessions in about a minute.

  1. 01 Install it npx k0-board — it runs on your machine, next to Claude Code.
  2. 02 Sign in GitHub or Google. Your board syncs to k0 Cloud and keeps working offline.
  3. 03 Open ten sessions Start them from the board and stop wondering which one needed you.
