Your board, on every machine

k0 Cloud keeps a copy of your Claude Code board: sign in with GitHub or Google, see it on every machine and on your phone — while files, diffs and commits never leave yours.

Since k0 0.6.0

What it is

Your board lives on your machine and keeps working with no network at all. k0 Cloud holds a copy of it, so it survives a lost laptop, reaches your other machines, and can be read from a browser at k0-board.com/app/ — on your phone too.

Every few seconds, while you are signed in and let in, k0 sends what changed and fetches what your other machines changed. The same repository checked out on two machines is one column, because the cloud names it by its origin remote, cleaned of any password.

What goes up, and what never leaves

Goes upStays on this machine
epics, stories, tasksyour files, diffs and commits
decisions, rounds, checkliststhe folder a session works in
dependencies and the Logwhich terminal window it is in
the sessions a story has had, and their statusthe commit a session started at

Sessions go up but do not come down: a session is a terminal on the machine that opened it. The cloud keeps its status so the web board can show it.

Why a key needs the cloud

A key is one number for your whole account: K150 is the same story in every repository and on every machine. Two machines can only agree on what comes next in one place, so the number comes from the cloud the moment you create a story.

When the cloud does not answer within two and a half seconds, the story is made anyway with a provisional key, K~3. It works everywhere a key does, and as soon as the cloud is back it is given its real number, oldest first.

How to use it

  1. Open the board and choose GitHub or Google on Connect your account — or run k0-board login. There is no password of k0's own.
  2. k0 is in early access: until your account is approved the board says You are on the list and sends nothing. It notices the approval by itself.
  3. If the board already had stories, it offers Move your board to k0 Cloud and shows every old key beside its new one before anything changes. A backup is written first.
  4. Watch the chip at the end of the top bar: green, everything is up; amber, changes are waiting; grey, the cloud cannot be reached and the board carries on by itself.
  5. Open k0-board.com/app/ anywhere to read the board. It is read-only: the board is edited from k0 on your computer.

Small print

Sign out, or k0-board logout, leaves the board exactly as it is and stops syncing. K0_CLOUD=off switches the cloud off entirely.
