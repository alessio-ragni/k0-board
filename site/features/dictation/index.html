Dictation that stays on your Mac

Offline dictation on macOS 26 in place of Wispr Flow — hold Control, talk to Claude Code, let go. Nothing you say leaves the Mac.

Since k0 0.8.0

Hold Control, speak, let go

k0 has a dictation of its own, offline, to replace Wispr Flow. Hold Control, say a phrase, let go, and the text is pasted wherever the cursor is — the terminal of a Claude Code session, a browser, a note. It needs macOS 26; anywhere else the page says so and the switch stays grey.

Why it matters

Talking to Claude Code is quicker than typing to it, and most dictation does it by sending your voice to somebody else's server. This one does not. Nothing you say leaves the Mac: the recording of a phrase is deleted once transcribed, and the last ten texts live in ~/.k0/dictate/history.json — never in the board's database, which is synced to k0 Cloud.

Nobody chooses a language either. Whisper hears which one you speak; Apple's engine listens in Italian and English at once and keeps the surer of the two.

Try it before you switch

Is an offline engine good enough, and which one? That is answered on your own Mac, with your own voice. Open http://k0.localhost:4319/dictate.html and dictate with Wispr as you always do: at the same moment k0 Dictate records the same microphone and gives it to both engines. Each phrase shows the three texts side by side, with how long each took from the instant you let go.

EngineWhat it needs
Applenothing — macOS fetches its models by itself
Whisperbrew install whisper-cpp, then a model of about 1.6 GB, once

macOS asks once for the Microphone and for Accessibility — the second is what lets k0 Dictate notice Control being held.

How to use it

  • On the trial page, press Keep Apple or Keep Whisper.
  • Quit Wispr Flow — with both running, both paste.
  • Press Switch dictation on. k0 Dictate starts now and at every login.
  • Hold Control on its own for a phrase. Control+Space, or Control tapped twice, is hands-free; Control again finishes and pastes.

Control with any other key — Control+C, Control+A — is a shortcut, and nothing happens. Escape drops the phrase.

The pill

A small pill at the bottom of the screen says where things stand, coloured so it is never taken for Wispr Flow's: light blue while it waits and listens, the bars moving with your voice; orange while the words are worked out; red for a second only when a phrase could not be heard or could not be pasted.

Right-click it for the last ten phrases, Paste last transcript for a paste that went nowhere, the microphone, and Reset dictation. The text arrives all at once when you finish, through the clipboard, and whatever was on the clipboard is put back a moment later.
